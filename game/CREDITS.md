## Code
- Phaser 4.2.1 (MIT), https://phaser.io

## Session 3 (art-v7)
- Room plates in `public/assets/rooms/` and Noor's dash/type sheets: rendered in Blender 4.2 (headless, Cycles) by `prompt-injection/art-v7/tools` from **Kenney Furniture Kit** and **Kenney Mini Characters** (CC0, kenney.nl).
