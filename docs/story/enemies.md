# Prompt Injection — Enemy Design

Every enemy has: (1) style matching its complaint, (2) clear tells (floor flash / wind-up pose / sound), (3) one move cracked only by a word swap on its plate, (4) a half-health change, (5) a punish window after its big attack.
Enemies speak only through plates. Copies (and fakes) cast no shadow.

## Phase 1 — Draft floor
### Paper Dummy (tutorial, corridor)
Plate "DUMMY IS STILL". Never attacks until swapped to "DUMMY IS QUICK" (teaches that swaps change enemies). Then one slow swipe to practise dodging.

### Scribble (basic mob, archive) — 3 hits
Crawling ink doodle. Lunge with 0.6s squash tell. Plate "INK IS WET": swap WET→DRY and it cracks, takes double damage. Half health: speeds up. Punish: 1s stuck after a missed lunge.

### Sticky Note swarm (mob, archive) — 1 hit each, groups of 4
Flutter and dive. Plate "NOTES STICK TOGETHER": swap TOGETHER→APART and the swarm scatters, diving one at a time. Punish: grounded after a dive.

### BOSS: The Draft (locked)
Unfinished work that was never sent. 3 health bars, ~25–30 Caret hits.
- Paper Swipe: two slashes, floor flashes first.
- REPHRASE Ring: shockwave, dash through it (i-frames).
- Crumple Toss: three paper balls in a spread.
- Guard plate "THE DRAFT IS STRONG": swap STRONG→WEAK (WEAK from a plate in the arena edge) to break guard.
- Half health: splits into two shadowless Drafts; the real one keeps the plate.
- Punish: 2s pause after REPHRASE Ring.
- Defeat drops the claim shard with the corrupted date.
