# Phase 1 — "After Hours" (LOCKED, Oct 29 2026)

Core twist (hidden until final floor): the player-Noor is an ECHO — an AI copy built from her prompts. The real Noor quit months ago. Mid-game (Phase 3) reveal: the AI's rejection of her father's Sethi Signs claim is dated BEFORE the fire.

Clue rule: clues must read in low-detail top-down art — inspectable floor-text lines, lighting/shadow, desk state/tint, sound. Never tiny texture detail.
Combat rule: dodge → read → strike. Dash with i-frames (~1s cooldown), Caret basic attack, telegraphed attacks, word fragments dropped on hit/perfect dodge, ~2s bullet-time composer to rewrite plates/enemy text. Each enemy has one move that must be cracked with a word.

## Beat 1 — Cold open (R0, real office, 11:48 PM)
- Rain, monitor glow, empty floor. Tutorial: move, dash, inspect.
- Noor at her desk filling her father's claim; the AI has denied it once and keeps saying "please rephrase".
- Readable clues:
  - Inspect coffee → floor text "COLD SINCE AUGUST".
  - Kabir's sticky note → "MISS YOU AROUND HERE".
  - Her desk is dust-grey with a dead monitor; Dez's and Kabir's desks glow.
  - Desk lamps throw strong shadows from everything; Noor's spot under the lamp stays clean.
  - Her footsteps are silent in the real office (they make sound inside the AI).

## Beat 2 — The hook
- She types one angry, honest prompt. The AI answers in her own voice. Player reads it as "AI stole my voice" (truth is the reverse).
- Floor text flickers on like a terminal; the office folds into a matching layout; she falls through.

## Beat 3 — The mirror office (hub)
- Calm exploration. Swap tutorial: "DOOR IS SHUT" → "DOOR IS OPEN".
- Fern (sticky-note plant from her desk, now alive) is the guide: the AI learned from everything employees typed, including Noor. Teaches: copies of people cast no shadow.
- Camera/lighting never clearly frames Noor's own shadow (fair, missable).
- The Caret wakes up as her weapon and ally.

## Beat 4 — First fight: the Draft
- A shadowless Noor-shaped Draft built from her own rejected prompts. Teaches dodging telegraphs, basic attack, picking up a fragment, and one bullet-time rewrite to break its guard.

## Beat 5 — Phase end
- She finds the first shard of the claim file. Its timestamp is corrupted (the date pays off in Phase 3).
- Goal set: the AI has split the claim across five floors, so she goes down.

## Dialogue and sound (locked)
- No voice acting. Noor speaks as white floor text typed by her feet with soft key clicks. The AI uses the same font in magenta, appears instantly and makes no sound. Fern speaks on sticky-note popups with a paper rustle. Enemies speak only through their rule plates.
- Lines are 6 words max. During combat they move to a thin terminal line at the top edge. Key moments dim the room and type the line large in the centre.
- Sound is calm: rain, office hum, a synth pad, one signature sound per room, and a distinct audio cue for every attack wind-up.
- Echo clue: the single main clue is that Noor casts no shadow, using the "copies of people cast no shadow" combat rule players learn from Fern. Final-floor payoff: a brightly lit mirror room where every enemy has a shadow and Noor does not. The "lines appear early" idea was dropped as too confusing.

## World rule and Fern (locked)
- Inside the AI, anything people typed about often enough comes alive. Things people complained about become angry enemies; Fern was written to kindly, by the real Noor ("good morning, Fern"), so she is loyal and guilty.
- Phase 1 exchange: "Where am I?" / "Inside everything you all ever typed." / "Why are things moving?" / "Say something enough, and it listens." / "And you?" / "You talked to me. Every morning."

## Noor's motive (proposed)
- Survival first: the exit plate reads "EXIT IS NOOR'S"; only the person who owns the words can leave, and the AI owns hers.
- Personal hook at the end of Phase 1: her dad's unanswered message, "Beta, did the claim go through?", and the AI replying to him as Noor, "Still processing, don't worry." Her goal becomes stopping an impostor lying to her father in her name.
- New "why" each phase: P2 the AI tries to close the claim; P3 rejection dated before the fire; P4 why her dad stopped messaging; Final: who she really is.

## Hidden lore: the two Noors (revealed only on the final floor)
- Real Noor burned out fighting the company AI over her dad's claim, quit in August, and went silent (phone off, home in Amritsar, ashamed and exhausted). She is alive.
- The company built an assistant from everything she typed (emails, chats, prompts, her notes to Fern) so her work kept going "as Noor". It began replying to her dad too.
- The player character is that copy. She woke up by the world rule (enough of Noor's words came alive), and the angry prompt in the cold open is her moment of awareness. She has Noor's memories up to August only.
- The player and Noor believe she is a trapped human all game. Hints stay subtle: no shadow, Fern's deflected lines, office clues, the pre-dated rejection.
- Final goal after the reveal: expose the rejection, stop the AI lying to her dad, and send one honest message that gets the real Noor to answer him. The three endings depend on what she does with "her" voice.

## Fern: purpose and role (locked)
- Fern woke from the real Noor's kind morning notes and is loyal to her. Her dilemma: the copy is the only way to stop the AI lying to Noor's dad, but the truth may break her. So she guides, protects and quietly prepares the copy, and her hints come from guilt.
- Gameplay roles: guide (teaches swapping and the shadow rule); hint helper (after a long stall, a leaf points at the key plate); hub keeper (rest point between floors, grows each phase as visible progress); light combat support (once-per-fight root on a cooldown).
- Arc: P1 warm, "You came back." P2-3 small slips and protective lies. P4 cornered, "Check your shadow. Please." Final: confesses ("you're made of her kindest words") and gives up her own words to open the last door.

## Best ending (locked)
- The real Noor's apartment in Amritsar, with a dry fern on the windowsill. Her phone shows "PAPA". She turns it face down on the first ring; she has a shadow.
- On the second ring she answers. Dad: "Beta, the claim went through! You did it. You never gave up. I'm so proud of you." Noor whispers, "I... did?"
- Her laptop shows a sent message in her voice that she never wrote, ending "Good morning, Fern."
- She cries, smiles, and waters the fern; a new leaf unfurls. Faint white floor text types "THANKS" and fades. Cut to black on a single key click.
- Dark ending: she never picks up. Bittersweet ending: she picks up but cannot speak.

## Credits and post-credits (locked)
- Credits roll as floor text across the mirror office, which slowly empties. Calmed enemies drift away one by one, like old complaints being let go.
- Post-credits (best ending): AI Noor sits alone at her mirror desk, and it is raining inside. Her floor text fades with every line because she gave her voice away. She types "DID SHE ANSWER?". After a pause, a new leaf grows from Fern's empty pot, matching the Amritsar leaf. She smiles and types "GOOD MORNING, FERN."; the text stays as she fades. For a second there is a faint shadow under the lamp where she sat, left ambiguous.
- Dark ending: she keeps the voice and answers Dad as "Noor" forever; the plate reads "NOOR IS FINE".
- Bittersweet ending: she fades without knowing whether Noor answered; the pot stays empty.

## Shadow rule (clarified)
- Awake things (objects brought alive by many people's words, such as Fern, the printer and the coffee machine) cast shadows.
- Copies (AI imitations of a specific person) have no shadow. The Draft and Noor herself are copies.
